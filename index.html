The only reason any of this is up is to keep these mods alive and available. SPT is shutting down, mods get taken offline, authors move on, and once a mod is gone it's gone for good. I'd rather people could still play with the stuff everyone spent years building.

Same if you just want something changed instead: credit fixed, description corrected, a link pointed at your page rather than mine. Prefer to keep it off a public issue? Say so in it and I'll follow up privately.
